Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Applies to https://subnet.mohit-tools.com/

1. Zero-Upload Client-Side Subnet Processing

Mohit Tools respects your operational privacy. All IPv4 addresses, CIDR prefixes, subnet masks, and binary bit modifications you enter on subnet.mohit-tools.com are computed 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not collect, log, store, or transmit your entered IP addresses or network configurations to any backend server.

2. Browser Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your Light/Dark interface theme preference (mohit-subnet-theme). This preference remains strictly on your device and can be cleared anytime through your browser settings.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate, anonymized traffic metrics such as page visits, browser types, and general geographic regions so we can improve tool performance. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect usage statistics without identifying individual users personally.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep our engineering tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
